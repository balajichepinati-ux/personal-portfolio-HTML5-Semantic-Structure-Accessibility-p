Biography

Hey there! I am Alex Mercer. I began my journey in engineering with a strong fascination for interactive graphic visuals. Over the years, I discovered that too many spectacular 3D experiences lock out users with disabilities.

This discovery shifted my mission: **I synthesize advanced visual arts with strict accessibility compliance.** By focusing on semantic HTML5 structure, customized styling logic, and modern keyboard traps, my web apps achieve perfect utility alongside stunning aesthetics.

When I'm not writing layouts, I audit digital interfaces, consult for high-growth SaaS startups on WCAG compliance, and create open-source interactive canvas solutions.
